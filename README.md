# Eco-Mythbusters Roundtable

**Eco-Mythbusters Roundtable** is a youth-led digital platform dedicated to challenging common environmental misconceptions through evidence-based blogs and expert roundtable discussions.

**Website:** https://ecomythbustersrt.github.io/ecomythbustersroundtable/

---

## About

Environmental issues are often surrounded by myths, oversimplifications, and trend-driven claims. Eco-Mythbusters Roundtable aims to move beyond these narratives by combining rigorous research with thoughtful discussion.

The project consists of:

- **Analytical Blogs** — Each article investigates a common sustainability myth using scientific evidence, data, and critical analysis.
- **Evaluative Podcasts** — Every podcast builds upon a blog by bringing together peers, local voices, and professionals, including environmental scientists, NGO workers, policymakers, and technology innovators.

Together, they provide a more nuanced understanding of environmental challenges and solutions.

---

## Our Purpose

Our mission is to help young people and communities make better-informed sustainability decisions by:

- Debunking common environmental myths
- Promoting critical thinking and scientific literacy
- Amplifying expert and community perspectives
- Connecting environmental issues to real-world challenges and solutions
